I am a third-year PhD Candidate at MIT and an NSF Graduate Research Fellow. I study energy economics, with a focus on the structural changes to the U.S. economy necessary for the transition to clean energy. I use tools from industrial organization, public finance, and trade, as well as power systems engineering.

Previously, I worked at the White House Council of Economic Advisers, where I covered the climate and industrial strategy portfolios. I received my BA from Yale in 2023.
